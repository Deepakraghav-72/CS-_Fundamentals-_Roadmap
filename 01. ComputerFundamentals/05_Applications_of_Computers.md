# 💻 COMPUTER FUNDAMENTALS

# 05. APPLICATIONS OF COMPUTERS

> **Learning Goal:** Understand how computers are used in different industries, organizations and everyday life.

---

## 1. Introduction

Aaj ke digital world mein computers almost har field mein use hote hain.

Computers ki help se hum:

- Complex calculations kar sakte hain.
- Large amounts of data store kar sakte hain.
- Information quickly share kar sakte hain.
- Business operations manage kar sakte hain.
- Scientific research kar sakte hain.
- Automation aur online services use kar sakte hain.

**Definition:** Applications of computers refer to the different areas and purposes in which computers are used to perform tasks, process data and solve problems.

## 6. Challenges and Risks

Computers ke applications ke saath kuch risks bhi hote hain.

### 1. Cybersecurity Threats

Hacking, malware, ransomware aur phishing jaise risks.

### 2. Privacy Issues

Personal information ka unauthorized access ya misuse.

### 3. Data Loss

Hardware failure, accidental deletion ya cyberattacks se data loss ho sakta hai.

### 4. Technology Dependency

Excessive dependency se manual skills aur independent working affect ho sakti hai.

### 5. Job Displacement

Automation kuch repetitive jobs ko reduce ya transform kar sakti hai, lekin naye technical roles bhi create hote hain.

### 6. Digital Divide

Har person ke paas internet, computer aur digital skills available nahi hote.

### 7. Health Issues

Long screen time se eye strain, poor posture aur physical inactivity ho sakti hai.

## 8. Important Interview Questions & Answers

### Q1. What are the applications of computers?

**Answer:** Applications of computers refer to the different areas where computers are used, such as education, banking, healthcare, business, communication, research and entertainment.

### Q2. How are computers used in banking?

**Answer:** Computers are used for account management, online banking, ATM operations, UPI transactions, payment processing and fraud detection.

### Q3. How are computers used in education?

**Answer:** Computers support online learning, digital classrooms, examinations, educational software and research.

### Q4. What is the role of computers in healthcare?

**Answer:** Computers help manage patient records, medical imaging, laboratory reports, patient monitoring and telemedicine.

### Q5. How are computers used in agriculture?

**Answer:** Computers help with weather forecasting, crop monitoring, irrigation management, soil analysis and agricultural research.

### Q6. How are computers used in communication?

**Answer:** Computers support email, instant messaging, video conferencing, voice calls and file sharing.

### Q7. What is the role of computers in business?

**Answer:** Computers help businesses with accounting, billing, inventory management, customer management, sales analysis and online transactions.

### Q8. How are computers used in transportation?

**Answer:** Computers are used in ticket booking, GPS navigation, traffic management, railway reservations and vehicle tracking.

### Q9. How are computers used in scientific research?

**Answer:** Computers perform complex calculations, data analysis, simulations, mathematical modelling and scientific research.

### Q10. How are computers used in software development?

**Answer:** Computers are used to write, compile or execute, test, debug, manage and deploy software applications.

### Q11. What is digital divide?

**Answer:** Digital divide refers to the gap between people who have access to digital technologies and those who do not.

### Q12. What are the risks of using computers?

**Answer:** Major risks include cybersecurity threats, privacy issues, data loss, health problems and excessive technology dependency.
